# The Strategy Deck

Thirty-four cards for executives choosing where to invest in AI, distilled from
the AI-Driven Business Innovation masterclass — the grown-up version of the
course's 12 capability cards. Free product: no gate, no capture.

**Live:** https://michael-borck.github.io/strategy-deck/

**Fund what it multiplies, not just what it replaces.**

## The design

One organic size: 34 cards — Frame (5), Foundations (6), Balance (4), Invest
(9), Adopt (5), Decide (5). One card type: why it goes wrong, the move, the
line.
